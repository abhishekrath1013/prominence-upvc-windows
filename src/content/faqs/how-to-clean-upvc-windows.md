---
question: "How do I clean uPVC windows?"
category: maintenance
order: 10
---

uPVC windows are built to need virtually no maintenance. For routine cleaning, a purpose-made uPVC cleaning solution is the safest choice. If you'd rather use a homemade mix, one cup of vinegar in four cups of hot water works — spray it on, wipe away dirt, then buff dry with a cloth. Use vinegar sparingly though: its acetic acid can shorten the frame's lifespan with repeated use, so a proper uPVC cleaner is better for the long run.
