question: "How are uPVC windows made?"
category: technical
order: 15

More goes into it than most people expect — from raw material selection through to final packaging. Raw material grade and extrusion temperature largely determine the profile's quality and lifespan; precision cutting and fusion-welding machinery, run by trained technicians, shape and join the frame; and the glass, gaskets, hardware and sealant used all affect how the finished window performs for years afterward. Every stage matters, which is why it's worth choosing a manufacturer with real experience behind it.
