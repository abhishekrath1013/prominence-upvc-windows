---
question: "What are double-glazed uPVC windows?"
category: performance
order: 5
---

A double-glazed uPVC window has two glass panes mounted in the same sash, separated by a spacer and a layer of insulating gas. That gap is what does the work — it cuts down heat transfer, sound transmission and UV radiation far more effectively than a single pane, which is why double glazing is the most common upgrade homeowners ask for.
