---
question: "What is uPVC?"
category: general
order: 1
---

uPVC stands for **unplasticized polyvinyl chloride** — a rigid polymer, not the flexible PVC used in pipes or flooring. It's self-extinguishing, so it resists fire rather than feeding it; highly thermal-resistant, so it doesn't transfer outdoor heat or cold into your room; and strong enough for tall structures despite being much lighter than aluminium or timber.
