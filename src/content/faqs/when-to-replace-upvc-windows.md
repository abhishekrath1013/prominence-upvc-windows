---
question: "When should I replace my uPVC windows?"
category: maintenance
order: 13
---

Quality uPVC windows carry long warranty periods, and well-made ones can last a couple of decades. Over many years of weather exposure, hardware may need minor adjustment, or a sash may sag slightly — that's usually a repair, not a replacement job. How long yours lasts before replacement becomes necessary comes down largely to the raw material quality behind it, which is why checking warranty terms at purchase time matters.
