category: general
order: 18

uPVC windows are manufactured all over the world, so origin alone doesn't tell you much — what matters is which standards the product has actually passed. Windows built to EN standards go through rigorous testing for wind load, water resistance and UV protection. When comparing manufacturers, ask what certifications and test results back up their claims, not just where the factory is.
