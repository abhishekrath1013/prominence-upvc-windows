uPVC typically costs less upfront than a comparable aluminium or timber window, and the savings continue afterward — better insulation lowers your energy bills, and the frame is virtually maintenance-free, so you're not budgeting for repainting or repairs down the line. For an exact number, request a quote for your specific openings and requirements.
