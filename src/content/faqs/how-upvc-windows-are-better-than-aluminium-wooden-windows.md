---
question: "How are uPVC windows better than aluminium or wooden windows?"
category: buying
order: 6
---

Wood swells and warps after prolonged exposure to rain, which slowly undermines its insulation and its seal. Aluminium solved the warping problem but conducts heat readily, so it performs poorly on energy efficiency. uPVC was engineered to close both gaps at once: it's self-extinguishing, highly thermal-resistant, strong despite being lightweight, and available in a wide range of colours and finishes — without timber's maintenance or aluminium's heat transfer.
