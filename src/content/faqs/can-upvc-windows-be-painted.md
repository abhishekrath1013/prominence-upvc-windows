---
question: "Can uPVC windows be painted?"
category: maintenance
order: 12
---

Technically yes, using a specially formulated uPVC paint — but we don't recommend it. Standard spray paint compromises the surface, and painting works against the entire point of uPVC: a frame that stays clean without upkeep. If you want a colour other than white, laminate finishes (like our wood and contemporary options) achieve it properly, without touching the window's low-maintenance performance.
