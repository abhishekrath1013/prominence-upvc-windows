---
question: "Are uPVC windows toxic or harmful to the environment?"
category: general
order: 17
---

No. uPVC was specifically developed as a cleaner alternative to older PVC formulations that used lead and other harmful additives. It's a recyclable thermoplastic that keeps material out of landfills and avoids groundwater contamination associated with older plastics. Prominance profiles are additionally 100% lead-free, using a Calcium-Zinc based formulation, and are RoHS-verified by SGS with no lead detected.
