---
question: "How long do uPVC windows last?"
category: buying
order: 7
---

That depends on the manufacturer — a uPVC window can last anywhere from a few years to a couple of decades, depending on the raw material, steel reinforcement and hardware used. Before buying, always check what warranty a manufacturer is willing to put behind their windows; the warranty terms tell you more about real-world lifespan than any general claim about the material.
